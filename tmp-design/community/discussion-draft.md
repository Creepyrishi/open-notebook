## Main post

**Title:** We're redesigning Open Notebook's UI — tell us what resonates (not a vote!)

Open Notebook grew a lot faster than its interface did. The product got
multi-provider AI, podcasts, transformations, i18n, self-hosting — but the UI
is still, honestly, a stock component library with default settings. Pale,
generic, and in some screens (Models, we see you) genuinely hard to use.

We're starting a full redesign: visual identity first, then screen-by-screen
restructuring, then navigation/UX. Before any code is written, we want your
eyes on the direction.

### What we made

We designed **four aesthetic directions** and executed each one as a
high-fidelity mockup of the same screen — the notebook workspace — with
hand-designed light **and** dark themes:

| | Direction | Thesis |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Warm Library / Editorial** | Paper and ink. A calm, literate research studio — serif display type, parchment and espresso tones. |
| B | **Refined Technical Tool** | Linear/Raycast-grade precision. Dark-first, warm graphite, one electric accent, monospace doing real work. |
| C | **Alive & Colorful** | Color as protagonist. Each content type owns a hue; tinted panels; the joyful opposite of a gray dashboard. |
| D | **Calm Clean / Soft Neutral** | Maximum restraint with an identity: soft elevation instead of borders, quiet functional pastels, warm-neutral canvas. |

### What we're asking

**This is not a vote.** We're not going to tally reactions and ship the
winner. The final direction will almost certainly be a hybrid, and the
maintainers will make the call — what we need from you is the *texture* of
your reaction:

1. **Which of these feels like "Open Notebook" to you — and why?**
2. For each direction that provokes a reaction: **what specifically works,
   what specifically doesn't?** Typography, color, density, dark mode,
   anything.
3. **Which one would you want to look at every day, for hours?** (Different
   question from "which is prettiest.")
4. **What would you steal from one direction into another?**

Please reply **inside the comment thread of the direction** you're commenting
on (pinned below) — general/cross-direction thoughts go directly in the main
thread.

We'll keep this open for about two weeks, then post a synthesis of what we
heard and the direction we're taking. The full problem inventory and vision
document is here: PLACEHOLDER_LINK_VISION_DOC
