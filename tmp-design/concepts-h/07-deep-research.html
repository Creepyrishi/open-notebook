The honest verdict is both, in different places. The strong claim — that human attention capacity has biologically shrunk — is not supported anywhere in your library. What is well supported is a behavioral shift: attention is being spent in ever-smaller slices. Hari is careful on exactly this point; his argument is environmental capture, not neurological decay 1.

The measured evidence is about fragmentation, not capacity. Newport's case rests on switch costs — “attention residue” means each interrupted block underperforms, so fragmented days feel like shrinking spans even when the underlying faculty is intact 2. The video supplies the motive: feeds are tuned for retention in millisecond auctions, so shorter slices are a design outcome, not a symptom 3. A newly found study adds the missing primary data — screen-focus intervals among knowledge workers fell from around 2.5 minutes in 2004 to 47 seconds — measured behavior, on work devices, over two decades 4.

So the moral-panic framing and the crisis framing are both half right: capacity looks stable, but the environment now spends it in 47-second increments — which is a real, measurable loss even without any biological decline ~ synthesis.

Deep Research: takes longer · searches beyond the selected context
Spec · deep research
01 · Mode switcher Two efforts, one control, in the composer where the choice is made — neutral shell; the active value carries the teal dot and underline. Focused = fast, predictable, pre-curated context. Deep = investigates. The consequence line under the composer is the whole disclosure — no modal, no wizard.
02 · Per-question promotion The bolt beside send runs a single question deep without converting the session — the session default stays Focused; the promoted question carries its own quiet “deep research” tag under the bubble.
03 · Activity, not a tool log Default level = significant events, summarized: searched → found → opened → comparing — each event class wears its own dot (search teal, open sage, compare violet — the derived hue, distinct from audio mauve). The grouped log (queries, sources, discards, elapsed) lives one click deeper, shown open here. The model’s private reasoning is never shown at any level.
04 · Permission to reach out External reach is opt-in, per request — the card wears the dashed slate edge of external evidence. The prompt says what it wants and why, offers “Allow once” or “Not in this session”, and declining just narrows the research — it never fails it.
05 · Always interruptible Quiet controls on the activity block: Stop discards, “Answer with what you have” salvages the evidence gathered so far into a normal cited answer, Refine… redirects mid-flight.
06 · Budget, only when relevant A tiny mono hint — elapsed time and step budget — appears while running and collapses into the summary strip when done. Never a progress bar pretending to know the future.
07 · Same citations, more effort The finished answer is dressed exactly like a Focused one — numbered chips, dashed external find with its “Add as source” story, honest ~synthesis, the receipt strip. Citation quality does not change between modes; only the effort allowed.
08 · Graceful degradation A local model without tool calling simply can’t investigate — Deep goes quiet, Focused remains. A hint, never an error: FocusedDeep Research llama-local · no tool calling — Deep Research unavailable with this model
